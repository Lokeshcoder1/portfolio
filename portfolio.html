Time-Series Forecasting · MLOps · Microservice

Energy Demand Forecasting — End-to-End Time-Series Regression Microservice

Personal Project · Python, XGBoost, LightGBM, FastAPI, Streamlit, Docker, Kubernetes

Data Pipeline

Time-aware preprocessing on real PJM Interconnection hourly energy data. Calendar features, 24h/168h lag features, 7-day rolling statistics, and weather integration — all saved as versioned artifacts for reproducible inference.

Model Training

Expanding-window time-series cross-validation across Ridge/Lasso, Random Forest, LightGBM, XGBoost, and LSTM baselines. Hyperparameter tuning via GridSearchCV.

FastAPI microservice with /predict (24h forecast), /predict-range (custom date range), /health, and /info endpoints. Pydantic validation for datetime ranges and numeric inputs.

Multi-page Streamlit app with 7-day load forecasts, confidence intervals, peak-hour alerts, and custom weather upload for scenario-based forecasting. Kubernetes deployment with weekly retraining schedule.
